# Game Jam 2026

An arcade game where the player, as a raccoon crossing guard, directs traffic through an intersection that grows busier until it collapses into a pile-up.

## Language

### The player

**Raccoon**:
The player-controlled character who stands in the intersection and directs traffic by hand.
_Avoid_: Player character, guard, cursor

**Stop**:
The raccoon's paw-up signal that halts the lane it is facing.
_Avoid_: Halt, block, red

**Go**:
The raccoon's wave that sends the lane it is facing through, faster than it would move on its own.
_Avoid_: Wave-through, release, green

**Dash**:
A short burst of raccoon speed for reaching a lane in time.
_Avoid_: Sprint, run (reserved for a play session), boost

### Play session

**Run**:
One endless play session, from the first car to gridlock.
_Avoid_: Level, round, shift, game

**Rush hour**:
The escalation of traffic density over the course of a run, with no ceiling.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, wave, level

**Gridlock**:
The end of a run: wreckage has blocked the intersection so badly that traffic can no longer flow.
_Avoid_: Game over, death, loss

**Combo**:
The multiplier built by consecutive cars getting through safely; a crash resets it.
_Avoid_: Streak, chain, multiplier (as a noun for the mechanic)

### Accidents

**Crash**:
A collision between vehicles; the game's comedic payoff and the seed of the player's downfall.
_Avoid_: Accident, collision, wreck (as a verb)

**Wreckage**:
Crashed vehicles left in place as obstacles until cleared, making further crashes more likely.
_Avoid_: Debris, obstacles, wreck
